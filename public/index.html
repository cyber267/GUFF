🔥 Firebase Integration (Project: guff-a1491)

To link your native Android app with your Firebase project guff-a1491:

  • 1
    Open Firebase Console > guff-a1491 and click Add App > Android.
  • 2
    Enter the Android Package Name: com.aistudio.guff.chat and app nickname: Guff.
  • 3
    Download google-services.json and place it inside the app/ folder of the project.
  • 4
    To deploy this Hosting site, run firebase deploy --only hosting --project guff-a1491.
